## Cardboard box listing

`public/images/cardboard-box.webp` was generated using the built-in image generation tool for the fictional $1 listing, then encoded as WebP. It is not a photograph of real inventory.

Prompt (built-in image generation): “Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: photograph for a humorous fictional $1 real estate listing. Primary request: a literal ordinary brown corrugated cardboard moving box, open at the top with four flaps, sitting alone on a simple neutral floor against a warm off-white backdrop. Photorealistic product photography, visible cardboard grain and folded seams, natural soft daylight. Landscape 3:2 composition with the entire box visible and centered, enough room around it for a listing-card crop. It is just a cardboard box, not a house shaped like a box. No people, furnishings, logos, writing, text, price tags or watermark.”
